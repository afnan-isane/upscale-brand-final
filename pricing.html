FAQ

Pricing questions answered

Are media advertising spends included in management fees?
No. Ad budgets (such as Google Ads or Meta Ads spend) are paid directly to the ad platforms on your account card. Our management fee covers strategy, setup, creative design, copywriting, and ongoing optimization.
Can we customize our service scope?
Yes. If your business needs a specific combination of services (e.g. website redesign + local SEO), we create a tailored proposal aligned with your exact goals.
Who owns our ad accounts and creative assets?
You maintain 100% ownership of all accounts, domain access, graphics, website source code, and campaign data.
